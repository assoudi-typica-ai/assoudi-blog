Hi, I'm **Hicham Assoudi**, Oracle ACE Pro, Oracle consultant, computer science professor, AI researcher, and technical author.

I have spent more than 30 years working with Oracle technologies, from enterprise architecture and integration to databases, cloud platforms, and AI solutions. My professional journey includes working with organizations such as Oracle and IBM.

Alongside my industry career, I earned a **PhD in Computer Science**, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP). Today, I combine consulting, teaching, and applied AI research.

This combination of industry experience and academic research shapes how I approach technology: **build, experiment, evaluate, and understand the trade-offs before making recommendations.**

## Why this blog?

I created **assoudi.blog** to share what I build, test, and learn at the intersection of Oracle technologies and artificial intelligence.

My goal is to help Oracle developers, DBAs, architects, consultants, and technical decision-makers move beyond product announcements and AI hype toward practical, technically sound implementations.

Here you'll find hands-on articles, working code, experiments, and lessons learned covering:

- **Oracle Database AI:** AI Vector Search, semantic retrieval, multimodal search, and in-database machine learning.
- **AI models inside Oracle:** ONNX models, embeddings, inference, and SQL-based AI workflows.
- **OCI and AI engineering:** OCI Data Science, model training, fine-tuning, deployment, and integration.
- **Enterprise AI applications:** RAG, AI agents, NLP, and practical implementation patterns.
- **Architecture and evaluation:** Technical trade-offs, benchmarking, model performance, and production considerations.

I try to explain not only *how* something works, but also **when it makes sense to use it, where its limitations are, and what the alternatives might be.**

## Beyond the blog

I also write technical books and share code, experiments, models, and supporting resources through GitHub, Hugging Face, and other platforms.

Through my consulting work, I help organizations explore, architect, and implement Oracle and AI solutions, drawing on both enterprise experience and applied research.
